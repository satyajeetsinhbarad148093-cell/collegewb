Welcome to Marwadi University

Marwadi University is an educational institution located in Rajkot, Gujarat, India. It provides students with opportunities to develop academic knowledge, practical skills and professional abilities.

Our goal is to encourage innovation, creativity, leadership and lifelong learning through quality education and a supportive learning environment.
